### Email Design (Credit: Mathew Kang)

__**Subject line**__: Recruitment to Participate in a Survey About Linguistic Indifference

Dear [intended participant/s],

We are emailing you to invite you to be part of our research project about students’ attitudes on academic close reading, language’s role as a device for learning, and academic AI use.

Our goal of this new study is to see how linguistically indifferent American college students are. In other words, how closely do you pay attention when reading something handed to you in class or just in everyday life? 

While you will not receive compensation for the study, our hope is that, through your responses, we can uncover gaps in the way that professors teach core literary skills. Your participation will also help us make a standardized scale that will measure students’ ability to maintain close attention to language, with the goal of improving the quality of education in the future university.

Who can participate?
* American undergraduate students currently attending Emory University
* All genders, races and ethnic groups (inclusive of women and minorities)
* Those who are able to take about 30 minutes out of their day to complete our one-time survey

If you fit these requirements and are interested in helping, sign up for the study by clicking the link here.

Thank you for helping to improve the quality of education for all future students in the United States.
